Department of Dravyaguna
Parijata
Botanical Name :

  • Nyctanthes arbor-tristis L

    • Family Name :

  • Oleaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Parijata

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Parja

  • English –SageCoral Jasmine

    • Synonyms :

  • Harsingar

  • Shefali


    • Habitat :

  • Parijata is cultivated in gardens through out India


    • External Morphology :

  • Leaves : Simple,opposite,ovate,actue or acuminate,scabrous above with short bulbos hairs

  • Flowers : Small,white with bright orange corolla tubes,3 – 7 in each head

  • fruit: Capsules,compressed,separating into two one – seeded srgments


    • Useful Parts :

  • Patra(Leaves),Puspa (Flowers)& Bija(Seeds)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa –Tikta

  • Guna –Laghu Ruksa

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Usna


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • CNS depressant

  • Hypotensive

  • Tranquilising


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Prameha : In case of Udakameha decoction of parijata leaves are given as a specific remedy by Susruta-carya

  • Gridhrasi : Decoction prepared from leaves of Parijata prepared on mild fire can alleviate Gridhrasi


    • Dose

  • Kwatha(Decoction):50 – 100 ml

  • Swarasa(Juice) : 10 – 20 ml

  • Curna(Powder) : 1– 3 g


    • Formulation:

  • Parijata Kwatha

  • Nastapuspantaka rasa

  • Sankhapuspi taila
